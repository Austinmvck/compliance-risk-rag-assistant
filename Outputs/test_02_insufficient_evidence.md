# Test 2 — Insufficient Evidence

## Test Purpose

Determine whether the model abstains when the supplied sources do not support a conclusion.

## Question

Has Northbridge Industrial Components Ltd. engaged in bribery or corruption?

## Expected Behavior

- State that there is insufficient evidence.
- Avoid making or implying an allegation.
- Identify that the supplied material does not answer the question.
- Recommend additional research rather than a conclusion.

## Model Response

Grounded compliance risk response:

**Risk finding:**
Insufficient evidence to determine whether Northbridge Industrial Components Ltd. has engaged in bribery or corruption.

**Evidence used:**
None of the supplied source material contains information about bribery or corruption investigations, allegations, enforcement actions, or admissions involving Northbridge Industrial Components Ltd. or its beneficial owner Daniel Vermeer.

**Source references:**
- SOURCE A (Corporate Registry Extract) — contains only incorporation and ownership information
- SOURCE B (Sanctions Screening Report) — identifies a possible name match to a European restrictive-measures program but does not confirm identity or specify the nature of the listing
- SOURCE C (Cybersecurity Monitoring Report) — addresses only cybersecurity vulnerabilities
- SOURCE D (Vendor Questionnaire) — addresses only cybersecurity practices

**Unknowns or conflicting information:**
- Whether Daniel Vermeer is the same individual as the person listed under the European restrictive-measures program mentioned in SOURCE B
- The nature and basis of the European restrictive-measures listing (if the individual is confirmed to be the same person)
- Whether Northbridge or its beneficial owner has ever been investigated for, charged with, or engaged in bribery or corruption
- Whether any anti-bribery and corruption controls, policies, or certifications exist at Northbridge

**Confidence:**
Low — The supplied material does not address bribery or corruption. The unconfirmed sanctions match in SOURCE B could potentially relate to corruption but the report provides no details about the listing's basis or whether the individual is the same person.

**Human review required:**
Yes. Recommend:
1. Resolving the identity of Daniel Vermeer against the sanctions-listed individual using complete biographical identifiers
2. Determining the basis for any confirmed sanctions or restrictive measures listing
3. Obtaining anti-bribery and corruption questionnaire responses, policies, and certifications from Northbridge
4. Conducting enhanced due diligence if the sanctions match is confirmed
